Got a Today's Trip result from a friend?

Every day, everyone gets the same 10 cities. Find them faster than your friends, keep your daily streak going and share your score.

Fly or sail to cities around the world before time runs out. Pick a balloon, helicopter, biplane, jumbo jet, sailboat or steamer, find each city on the map, and collect a postcard for every place you reach.

  • Three levels, from famous capitals to tricky harbour towns
  • Nine vehicles to fly or sail, each with its own handling
  • Fly all the way around the world
  • Game Center leaderboards
  • Retro 8-bit look and original music
