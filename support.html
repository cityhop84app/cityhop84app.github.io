Help with City Hop

Found a bug, a city in the wrong place, or have an idea? Email support@playcityhop.com. I read every message and usually reply within a few days.

How do I play?

Pick a level, choose FLY or SAIL, pick a vehicle and press START. Steer with your finger towards the city named at the top. Reach it before the timer runs out. You can see the How to Play pages again under Settings.

How do I unlock more vehicles?

Score high or find enough cities in a level. The menu shows what each locked vehicle needs.

I bought Remove ads but still see ads

Open Settings in the game and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I change my ad privacy choice?

Open Settings in the game and tap Ad privacy. You can also turn off tracking in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Why isn't a city or border shown the way I expect?

The map follows UN-style borders and leaves out disputed areas. It's a game, not an official map, and we try to be accurate and neutral. If you think something is wrong, please let me know.
