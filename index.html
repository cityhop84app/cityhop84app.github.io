CITY HOP

An original retro geography game for iPhone and iPad.

Got a Today's Trip result from a friend?

Every day, everyone gets the same 10 cities. Find them faster than your friends, keep your daily streak going and share your score.

Fly or sail to cities around the world before time runs out. Pick a hot-air balloon, helicopter, zeppelin, biplane, seaplane or jet, or a sailboat, steamer or speedboat, find each city on the map, and collect a postcard for every place you reach.

  • Four levels, from famous cities to Expert with no country names
  • 255 world cities, each with its own pixel-art postcard
  • Nine vehicles to fly or sail, each with its own handling
  • Fly all the way around the world
  • Game Center leaderboards
  • Retro 8-bit look and original music
