PRIVACY POLICY

City Hop · Last updated 18 November 2026

City Hop is a game made by Jasper Vervoort, an independent developer in the Netherlands. This policy explains what happens with your data when you play. In short: I don't collect your personal data myself. The game shows ads from Google, and Google may use some device data for those ads, but only as you allow.

What the game stores on your device

Your best scores, the cities you've found, unlocked vehicles and your settings (sound, vibration and so on) are saved only on your own device. They are never sent to me. Deleting the app deletes them.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a full-screen ad after some games. These ads come from Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect data such as your device's advertising identifier, IP address, rough location, device type and how you interact with ads.

  • In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent before any personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings › Ad privacy in the game.
  • On iPhone and iPad Apple asks whether you allow tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", Google cannot use your device's advertising identifier.
  • If you don't consent, you may still see ads, but they won't be personalised.

Read how Google uses this data: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Remove ads purchase

You can buy "Remove ads" once. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. I don't receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. After the purchase no ads are loaded and Google receives no ad data from the game.

Game Center

If you're signed in to Apple Game Center, your scores are sent to Apple's leaderboards together with your Game Center nickname. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. You can sign out of Game Center in your device settings.

No accounts, no analytics

The game has no sign-up and no account of its own, and it does not use analytics or crash-reporting services of its own.

Children

City Hop is suitable for all ages, but it is not specifically directed at children under 13. I do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Parents can turn off ad tracking for their child's device in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking, or buy Remove ads.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, and to object to its use. Because I don't hold personal data about you, requests about ad data are best sent to Google, but you're always welcome to contact me and I'll help. You can also complain to the Dutch data protection authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
